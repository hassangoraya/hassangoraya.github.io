DTI · Hardware Hardware
3-Phase Motor Forward/Reverse Control (Auto Cycle)
Timer-based auto-cycle controlling a 3-phase induction motor through contactors on real hardware: forward 20s → 20s delay → reverse 20s → 20s delay, repeating until Stop. Chained TP (pulse) timers with elapsed-time (.ET) comparators hand off each stage cleanly, a TOF timer drives the loop-back bit, and Reset Time (RT) coils tied to Stop return the system to a clean state. Forward and reverse contactors are hardwired with interlocking to prevent a phase short-circuit.
TP / TOF Timers .ET Comparators Contactor Interlocking 3-Phase Motor
